Delete your SpendWise account and data

In the app, open More → Privacy & Account → Delete account and data. For security, sign out and sign in immediately before deletion.

Request deletion without the app

Email bytesforges@gmail.com with the subject “SpendWise account deletion”, preferably from the email linked to your account. Include your account email and state that you want your account and associated data deleted. Never send your password. We will verify ownership before processing your request.

Deletion removes your Firebase sign-in account and SpendWise cloud profile, expenses, bills, budgets, debts, savings goals, groups, friend records, split records and submitted AI reports. A temporary security marker blocks stale credentials; completed-deletion markers are scheduled for expiry after 24 hours. Provider backups and logs follow provider retention schedules.

Records independently kept by other people, and data already sent to DeepSeek or an external payment app, are not automatically removed. Tell us if your request also concerns those providers so we can explain the available steps. Clear app storage or uninstall to remove remaining local cache data.
