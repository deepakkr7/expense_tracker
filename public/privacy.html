SpendWise Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

SpendWise is an expense tracking and budgeting app. For privacy questions, contact bytesforges@gmail.com.

Information we process

Firebase Authentication processes your account email and authentication information. If you use Google sign-in, we receive your name, email and available profile photo. Your profile, income, expenses, bills, budgets, debts, savings goals, groups and split records are stored using Google Firebase Cloud Firestore to provide account synchronization.

If you grant contacts access, contacts are read on your device to help you choose friends. When you add a selected contact, their name and available email are saved to your account. We do not automatically upload your entire address book. Camera access supports QR scanning and receipt recognition. Receipt text recognition uses Google ML Kit on your device; SpendWise does not upload receipt images to its backend. Financial details you choose to save from a receipt are stored with your expenses.

SpendWise does not track your location. Notification permission enables expense and bill reminders. Notifications may display financial information on your lock screen; you can control visibility in Android settings.

Optional AI features

AI assistance is off by default. If you enable it in Privacy & Account, relevant income, debt, bills, expense descriptions, savings goal context and chat messages are sent through our Firebase backend to DeepSeek to generate responses. Do not submit bank credentials, card numbers or other unnecessary sensitive details. AI is general budgeting guidance and can be inaccurate. Disable AI at any time to stop new requests; disabling AI does not erase information already processed by the provider.

DeepSeek processes submitted information under its own privacy policy, including provider retention and international processing practices. Contact us with deletion questions involving submitted AI data. SpendWise does not save routine chat transcripts in Firestore. If you report an AI response, the selected response and your reason are saved for developer review for up to 90 days, subject to deletion processing.

Discovery and external apps

Email discovery is optional and off by default. When enabled, verified signed-in users who know your email may obtain your name, email and profile photo through the friend lookup feature. Your financial profile is not returned. Disabling discovery prevents new lookups; it does not erase records independently kept by others. UPI links open an external payment app; that app processes payments under its own terms. SpendWise does not verify that a payment has completed simply because a payment app was opened.

Providers, security and retention

Google Firebase provides authentication, cloud storage and backend processing; DeepSeek processes optional AI requests. Fonts are bundled with SpendWise and do not require a network request. Profile photos may load from their configured host. Requests use HTTPS. App preferences and Firestore caches may remain on your device; clear app storage or uninstall to remove remaining local data.

Cloud account records are retained until you delete them or request account deletion. Usage counters help prevent abuse and limit AI costs. After deletion, a temporary account marker prevents stale credentials from recreating data; completed-deletion markers are scheduled for expiry after 24 hours. Provider backups, operational logs and legal retention may follow the providers' retention schedules. We do not intentionally log AI prompts, responses, or authentication tokens in our application backend.

Deletion and choices

Use More → Privacy & Account → Delete account and data, or use our account deletion request page. Deletion removes your Firebase account and its SpendWise cloud records, including nested expenses, budgets, contacts, groups, savings goals and reports. It does not automatically remove independently kept records in another person's account or data already processed by external providers. Contact us for help with these requests.

You may deny camera, contacts and notification permissions through device settings. Manual expense entry remains available. SpendWise is intended for adults and is not directed to children. We may update this policy when our practices change and will update the date above.
